Questions

Good to know

How long does a membership last?

365 days from the day you join or renew.

Is my membership tax-deductible?

The Friends of the Irving Museums is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit (EIN 20-0591363). Contributions are tax-deductible as allowed by law; check with your tax advisor about the value of member benefits.

Can I pay monthly?

Yes, at the Supporting, Sustaining and Benefactor levels you can pay in 12 monthly installments. The yearly total is the same.

Can I pay by check or cash?

Yes. Print the paper membership form and mail it with a check to Friends of the Irving Museums, P.O. Box 170142, Irving, TX 75017-0142, or bring it to the Irving Archives & Museum at 801 W. Irving Blvd. Please don’t mail cash; bring it in person. Check and cash memberships can’t be paid in installments.

Who counts for the Student / Educator / Military / Senior rate?

Students, educators, active military, veterans, and seniors 65 and over all qualify for the $15 individual membership.

I’m already a member. How do I renew?

Choose your level above and check out the same way. Your new 365 days start when you renew.
